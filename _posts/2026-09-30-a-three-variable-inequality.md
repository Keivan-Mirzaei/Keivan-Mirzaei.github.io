For all real numbers $$x,y,z$$, prove that

$$
\begin{aligned}
&(x^2+yz)(y^2+zx)(z^2+xy)\\
&\qquad\leq (x^2+y^2)(y^2+z^2)(z^2+x^2).
\end{aligned}
$$

The variables may be positive, zero, or negative.
