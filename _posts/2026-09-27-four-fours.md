Use **exactly four copies of the digit 4** to make each integer from 1 through 10.

You may use addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, and parentheses. You may also join two adjacent fours to make `44`. Do not use square roots, factorials, decimal points, or any other digits.

For example, zero would be `(4 − 4) × (4 + 4)`, but zero is not one of your ten targets.
