Point on a webpage, drag to draw, and let your marks fade.

Laser Pointer requires macOS 12 or later and **Safari 17.5 or later**. This version is for Mac.

## Getting started

Open the Laser Pointer app and choose **Open Safari Settings…**. Enable the extension under **Extensions**.

On a regular webpage, click its Safari toolbar icon and choose **Use on this website**. After that, the toolbar icon toggles the laser directly on that website.

## Controls

- Move to point; drag to draw.
- Press **Space** to pause or resume normal page interaction.
- Press **Escape** to turn the laser off.
- Drag the gear to move it. Open it for three colours, three thicknesses, smoothing, and a 1–20 second visibility setting.

Marks remain while you keep writing, then fade after your chosen delay. Opening a new page clears the marks. Navigation never requests new website access automatically.

## Troubleshooting

If the toolbar icon is missing, check Safari Settings → Extensions and confirm Laser Pointer is enabled. If it cannot run on a website, click its icon to allow that website, or check Safari Settings → Websites → Extensions.

Safari's protected pages, Reader, and the built-in PDF viewer are not supported. Some websites use nested scrolling regions or unusual layouts that need individual testing.

To report a problem, [email Keivan Mirzaei](mailto:{{ site.email }}) with your macOS and Safari versions and a short description. Avoid including private webpage content or personal information in screenshots.
