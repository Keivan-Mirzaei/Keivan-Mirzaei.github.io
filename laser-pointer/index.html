A little extra help.

Where does Laser Pointer work? {% include icon.html name='chevron-down' %}

Restricted browser pages: For security, all browsers block extensions from running on internal pages (such as edge://, chrome://, about:, and new-tab pages), official extension stores (Microsoft Edge Add-ons, Chrome Web Store, Firefox Add-ons), Safari Reader view, and built-in PDF viewers. Switch to any regular https:// or http:// webpage to use the laser.

Website permissions: If the laser won’t start on a regular website, verify that site access is allowed:

  • Safari on Mac: Check Safari → Settings → Websites → Extensions (or Safari → Settings → Extensions → Laser Pointer) and allow the current website.
  • Safari on iPad: Tap the extensions icon in the address bar → Manage Extensions, or check Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions → Laser Pointer.
  • Edge & Chrome: Right-click the Laser Pointer toolbar icon, choose Manage extension, and check its Site access setting.
  • Firefox: Click the Extensions icon, open Manage Extension for Laser Pointer, and check the Permissions tab.
Can I still interact with the webpage? {% include icon.html name='chevron-down' %}

On desktop browsers, Laser Pointer places an active drawing layer over the page so dragging your mouse or trackpad draws smooth strokes instead of selecting text or dragging images.

Press Space at any time to pause the laser and interact with links, buttons, sliders, videos, or draggable graphs normally while your drawn marks stay visible on screen. Press Space again to resume drawing, or press Esc (or click the toolbar icon) to turn the laser off completely. On iPad, your fingers and trackpad always scroll and tap the page normally while Apple Pencil draws.

How does Apple Pencil work on iPad? {% include icon.html name='chevron-down' %}

On iPad, hovering the Pencil on a supported iPad moves the laser dot, and touching the screen draws, while finger touches and trackpad gestures continue scrolling, zooming, and clicking the page normally. A mode button beside the floating gear switches the Pencil between drawing and pointer-only mode, and a Turn off laser button in the settings panel lets you exit without a hardware keyboard.

Can I move the settings? {% include icon.html name='chevron-down' %}

Moving the settings gear: Drag the floating gear button to any corner of the browser window; it remembers its position locally and automatically closes its panel when you start drawing.
