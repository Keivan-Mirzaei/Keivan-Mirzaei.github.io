  1. Enable it in Safari.

    Once installed from the Mac App Store, open the app. Choose Open Safari Settings… and enable Laser Pointer under Extensions.

  2. Choose your website.

    On a webpage, click the toolbar icon, then Use on this website.

  3. Make your point.

    Move to point. Drag to draw. Press Space to pause and browse, or Esc to turn the laser off.

Allow each website when you choose. Pause stays with you as you follow links.
