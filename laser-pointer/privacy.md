Updated October 18, 2026

Laser Pointer has no advertising, analytics, app accounts or developer-operated data servers. It does not send webpage content or drawing activity to the developer.

Website access is requested only when you choose to use the laser on a website. The extension places a drawing layer over the page and reads page dimensions, scroll position and pointer events to keep marks aligned. It does not read or record page text, passwords or form entries.

Settings and gear position are stored locally. Whether the laser is on or paused is kept per tab for the browser session. Strokes stay in page memory until they fade, the page changes or the laser is turned off. The extension does not sync settings or marks.

Laser Pointer is purchased once through the Mac App Store. Apple handles the purchase, your App Store account and payment information under Apple's privacy terms. The app does not collect payment details, track a trial or support reminder, or send purchase records to the developer. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

You can revoke website access in Safari Settings → Extensions. No personal data is stored on developer-operated servers.
