Effective October 18, 2026.

Laser Pointer collects no personal data and sends no information to us or to third parties. It has no analytics, advertising, accounts, or network services.

Website access is requested only when you choose to use the laser on a website. The extension places a drawing layer over that page and reads page dimensions, scroll position, and pointer events to keep marks aligned. It does not read or record page text, passwords, or form entries.

Your settings and gear position are stored locally. Whether the laser is on is kept for each tab for the browser session. Strokes stay in page memory until they fade, the page changes, or you turn the laser off. Nothing is synced by the extension.

You can revoke website access in Safari Settings → Extensions. No data is stored on our servers.

For questions about privacy, [contact Keivan Mirzaei](mailto:{{ site.email }}). You can also visit [Laser Pointer support]({{ '/laser-pointer/' | relative_url }}).
