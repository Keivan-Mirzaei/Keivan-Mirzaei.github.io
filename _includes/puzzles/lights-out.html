Pressing a light flips that light and every light joined directly to it. Amber lights are on; hollow lights are off. Lines that cross make no extra connections.

Hint marks one useful light; press that light to use it. Tap Hint again to hide it. Numbers are optional.

Reset restores this puzzle's starting lights. Shuffle chooses another unsolved puzzle. Both keep your settings and can be undone.
