One light, many changes

Turn every light off. Pressing a light flips that light and every light joined directly to it. Filled amber lights are on; hollow lights are off.

Hover or focus to preview a move. Use the arrow keys to move between lights, then Enter or Space to press.

Hint marks one useful light. Press that light, or choose Use hint to make the move.

Each level has 1,000 numbered puzzles. Next puzzle and Shuffle skip solved puzzles. Choose a puzzle number to revisit any puzzle.

Lines that cross make no extra connections. Every puzzle has a solution.
