Changing board size or players starts a new game and clears Undo history. Reset keeps your settings and can be undone.

The saving preference applies to all puzzles on this device.

Choose an empty cell. Connect the two sides of your colour.

Red connects upper-right to lower-left; Blue connects upper-left to lower-right. Neighbouring stones connect when their cells share a side. Red begins, and stones stay put.

Undo takes back your move and the computer's reply, or one move when playing both colours. Reset clears the board and can be undone.

Use the arrow keys to move between empty cells, then Enter or Space to place a stone. Large boards can be scrolled on narrow screens.
