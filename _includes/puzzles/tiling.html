Drag a tile onto the floor, or choose it and tap where the marked square should go. The small dot marks the tile's anchor. The tray shows how many copies remain.

The horizontal tray scrolls with a swipe or trackpad. Use left and right arrow keys to browse tiles. On a phone, drag a tile vertically to place it. Settings also offers a compact grid tray.

Choose a placed tile to move it. Return tile, a double tap, or dragging off the floor sends it back to the tray.

Hint outlines one placement. Use Place hint, or tap the outlined anchor square. Tap Hint again to hide it.

Arrow keys move between floor squares; Enter or Space selects or places a tile. Delete returns a selected tile. Escape clears a selection or closes an open panel.

Reset clears this floor. Shuffle chooses an unsolved puzzle. Both keep your settings and can be undone. Tap the puzzle number to revisit any of the 1,000 puzzles at each difficulty.
