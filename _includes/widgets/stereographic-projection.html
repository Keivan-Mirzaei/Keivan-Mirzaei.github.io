From the plane to the sphere

The cube graph: 8 vertices, 12 edges, 6 faces. Drag Q to move its image P; drag elsewhere to rotate.

Static view: the amber faces correspond under stereographic projection.

Explore the correspondence

Amber marks the corresponding face, or the nearest vertex or edge to Q.

Drag Q freely on the plane. P follows on the sphere, and N, P, Q remain on one straight line. Moving Q farther from the point of tangency brings P closer to N. N represents the single point at infinity and belongs to the spherical image of the outside face.

Settings lets you hide either graph and emphasize matching vertices, edges, or faces. The plane shows only a finite window of the outside face. A face highlight follows Q; vertex and edge highlights follow the closest vertex or edge in the plane.

Drag elsewhere to rotate. Side view reveals the projection line; Above reveals the plane graph. Focus Q and use arrow keys to move it in the plane. Focus the scene and use arrows to rotate, or + and − to zoom. Undo and Redo reverse point and camera movements. Reset restores Q and the camera while preserving Settings.

The sphere has radius one, centre (0, 0, 1), and north pole N = (0, 0, 2). The plane is z = 0. Spherical edges and faces are obtained by stereographic projection of the planar drawing.
