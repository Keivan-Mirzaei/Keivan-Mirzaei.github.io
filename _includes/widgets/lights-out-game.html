Changing the board creates a fresh puzzle. Changing the goal restarts this board. Both changes can be undone.

Pressing a light flips that light and every light joined directly to it. Amber lights are on; hollow lights are off. Crossing lines create no extra connections. On a grid, neighbours are above, below, left, and right.

Hover or focus to preview a move. Use the arrow keys to move between lights, then Enter or Space to press. Undo takes back an action; Reset restores the starting lights.

Settings lets you compare all-off with complementing the start, or edit a starting pattern. Numbers appear while editing or following a solution. Show solution reveals a shortest press set; Another solution compares alternatives with the same parity.
