A random walk growing one step at a time

This silent 6.4-second video shows browser path 42 from the exploration
"A random walk at two scales". It starts at time zero and position zero.
A green line extends from left to right across a fixed grid. Each of its
256 steps advances time by 1/256 and changes the position by either
plus 1/16 or minus 1/16. The step counter advances to 256, then the final
path remains visible briefly. The horizontal axis runs from zero to one;
the vertical axis runs from minus two to two. There is no speech or music.

The interactive activity and static SVG figure in the article show the
same path. The downloadable Python example uses a different random
generator and therefore produces a different path for the same seed.
