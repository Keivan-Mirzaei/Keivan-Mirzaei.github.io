**How fast is a quantity changing? How much change has accumulated?** Calculus makes these questions precise. A **limit** describes approaching behavior, a **derivative** measures instantaneous change, and a **definite integral** measures accumulation. The **Fundamental Theorem of Calculus** explains how differentiation and integration are connected.

These notes develop the ideas together with their uses. You will learn to **calculate**, **interpret**, and **justify**: to find an answer, explain what it means, and establish why the reasoning is valid. Definitions, theorem hypotheses, and notation matter throughout the course.

The six chapters below give the route through Calculus I. **Begin with functions, domains, and representations.** The lesson outlines describe the planned course. The existing **derivatives lessons** are available for readers who already know limits and continuity; they will be refined as the course is developed.
