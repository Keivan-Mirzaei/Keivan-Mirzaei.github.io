Set the focus and break lengths above the timer, then start. Each phase waits for you to start the next. Changes to the lengths apply to your next session.

Show in top bar is on by default; turn it off in Settings. Pause or resume whenever you need to; Restart begins a new focus session.

Keep screen awake works while the timer runs and this page is visible, when your browser permits it.

Time-up sound is scheduled when you start the timer, so you can switch tabs or apps. Device sleep or browser restrictions can interrupt sound; missed alarms do not replay when you return.

Choose a background sound → Sound keeps playing independently, including during a break.
