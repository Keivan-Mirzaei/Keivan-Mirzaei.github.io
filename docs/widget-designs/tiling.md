# Floor tiling widget design sheet

**Status:** Reviewed and published on 4 October 2026, including both tray layouts and puzzle-specific saving and difficulty resets. [Live puzzle](https://keivan-mirzaei.com/puzzles/tiling/).

Follow the [widget roadmap](../widget-requirements.md) and the reviewed puzzle frame. This migration covers `/puzzles/tiling/`.

- **Name and stable ID:** Floor tiling; `tiling` in the registry and shared puzzle storage.
- **Purpose / interaction:** Fill every available floor square with all supplied tiles. Shapes translate without rotating or flipping. Missing squares are striped. Catalogue numbers and existing mathematical rules remain stable.
- **Layout options for review:** Compare a compact grid tray (beside the floor on wide screens, below it on phones) with a horizontally scrolling row beneath the floor. The scrolling row is the current default. Settings → Scroll tiles horizontally switches between them without changing the board, selection, or history. Retain the chosen preference when saving, resetting, shuffling, or clearing progress. Labelled controls follow the play area.
- **Appearance:** Use the shared interface font. Draw each shared floor/tile boundary once with a fixed screen-width stroke; omit grid lines inside a placed tile. Fill each placed tile as one continuous shape so resizing cannot expose seams between its squares. Use one continuous contour for tray shapes, drag ghosts, and placement previews, including concave corners. Keep a restrained colour palette and avoid numbering the tiles. A small dot marks each tray shape's anchor and a valid placement's anchor.
- **Controls:** Undo, Redo, Reset, Shuffle, Hint, Settings, Info. Return tile appears only for a selected placed tile. Place hint appears only with a visible hint. Next puzzle appears after completion when another unsolved floor is available.
- **Settings:** A labeled difficulty dropdown, Scroll tiles horizontally switch, and Save progress switch. Reset progress names the selected difficulty and confirms before clearing only that difficulty's Floor tiling boards, move history, and completion records. Start at puzzle 1. Preserve other difficulties' boards, histories, and solved records, plus the difficulty, tray layout and saving preference; leave other puzzles alone. Undo/Redo cannot restore the cleared progress.
- **Panels:** Settings, Info, and puzzle chooser start closed, open below the controls, and are mutually exclusive. Toggle using the same button or close with Escape. Outside taps and scrolling leave panels open. Closing a panel preserves the selection and board. Never save visibility.
- **Chooser:** A 1–1,000 slider previews the destination. Exact number entry remains useful for revisiting one particular puzzle among 1,000; Open puzzle commits. This is the same documented input exception as Lights Out.
- **Selection and placement:** A coloured outline highlights the chosen tray shape. A valid floor preview shows the full footprint and an anchor dot; invalid previews use dashed red edges plus a specific error on attempted placement. Dragging uses the same footprint and respects the grabbed square. Repeated shapes share a tray card with an exact copy count.
- **Hints:** Hint reveals one legal placement that can still complete the current board. Tap Hint again to hide it without placing. Place hint or tapping the outlined anchor applies it. Explain when current placements prevent a solution and invite Undo or Return tile. Clear stale hints after edits, history or navigation.
- **Completion:** Tint the floor frame and status green and announce “A perfect fit! Every floor square is filled.” Keep the different tile colours visible. Undo, Return tile or Reset clears the effect; Redo can restore it. Completion records persist during replay and Undo until Reset progress.
- **History:** A completed placement, move or return is one action. Reset, Shuffle, number navigation and difficulty changes are reversible with exact floors, placements and counts. Keep 100 compact snapshots without nesting histories. Invalid or unchanged actions leave history and Redo untouched. Migrate the current floor's existing Undo/Redo and preserve older generated-floor overrides.
- **Saving:** Shared storage with a separate Floor tiling preference on this device, enabled by default, retains boards independently at each difficulty and the action history. Turning saving off clears only Floor tiling's retained progress and stops future saves; the current game remains playable. Other puzzles retain their own preferences and progress. Selection, previews, hints, dragging and panels are transient.
- **Keyboard and phone:** Tap selection and placement are complete alternatives to dragging. The floor has one tab stop; arrows skip missing squares, Enter/Space select or place, Delete returns a selected tile. In the tray, Left/Right browses cards and scrolls the focused card into view. Horizontal touch gestures scroll the row; vertical drags place tiles. Escape clears a selection when no panel is open. Keep tray cards and controls at least 44 pixels. Scroll only the tray in row mode; the page and floor fit the viewport. Grid mode wraps cards. Seven-column Expert floors use the full available width on narrow phones, giving smaller floor cells. Respect reduced motion.
- **Typesetting:** Exact counts and simple floor dimensions such as 4 × 4 use the interface font. No formula renderer is needed.
- **Checks:** Solvability and unchanged catalogue IDs; legal/invalid/tap/drag placements; repeated copies; anchored dragging and cancellation; hint show/hide/use; completion and reversal; Reset/Shuffle/navigation histories; old/new saves; reset scope; keyboard focus; panels and layouts at phone widths and all four levels.
