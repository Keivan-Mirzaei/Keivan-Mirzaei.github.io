# Lagrange multipliers

- **Stable IDs:** Registry `lagrange-multipliers`; article anchor `lagrange-sections`.
- **Model:** `f=(x−2)²+4y²`, `g=x²+2y²`, with `g=1`. Both contour families are ellipses with different centres and axis ratios. The exact minimum is 1 at (1,0), with multiplier −1; the exact maximum is 9 at (−1,0), with multiplier 3.
- **Maps:** Two linked native SVG height fields with analytic contours, the feasible curve, and the same camera and coordinate scales. Height is compressed equally by 0.14. The shared Map dropdown also provides a top-down view. Cross-section plots and the curve profile are omitted as requested.
- **Arrows:** Dashed coloured arrows show unit gradient directions in the input plane. Solid coloured arrows lift those directions into the graph's tangent plane, encoding the maximum upward slopes. Black arrows lift the chosen unit direction. Top-down gradients use their actual magnitudes at a common scale.
- **Controls:** Position angle follows the ellipse; Minimum point and Maximum point select exact candidates. Direction from tangent and Follow ∇f / Follow ∇g control the chosen arrows. Dragging either 3D map rotates both without moving the point; dragging the top-down map moves the point. Settings provides keyboard camera sliders and Reset view.
- **Text:** Point, objective, and both gradients remain visible. Exact integer results have no approximation mark; other outputs use the shared three-significant-digit formatter. Only extrema show brief feedback, with the shared green completion effect at the minimum. Instructions are confined to the initially closed Info panel.
- **History and panels:** Shared Undo/Redo/Reset. One drag or slider gesture is one action; cancellation restores its prior state. Reset restores the starting point and direction while preserving map and camera. Settings and Info are mutually exclusive, remain open during outside interaction, and close with Escape. No progress saving.
- **Fallback and checks:** Three map previews and the identifying mark come from the shared model. Check feasibility, analytic gradients, directional derivative bounds, exact multiplier solutions, noncircular contours, projected arrow geometry, linked camera/history, keyboard use, phone layout, and entropy minimization.
