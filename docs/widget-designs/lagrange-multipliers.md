# Lagrange multipliers

- **Stable IDs:** Registry `lagrange-multipliers`; article anchor `lagrange-sections`.
- **Model:** `f=(x−2)²+4y²`, `g=x²+2y²`, with `g=1`. Both contour families are ellipses with different centres and axis ratios. The exact minimum is 1 at (1,0), with multiplier −1; the exact maximum is 9 at (−1,0), with multiplier 3.
- **Maps:** Two linked native SVG height fields with analytic contours, the feasible curve, and the same camera and coordinate scales. Height is compressed equally by 0.14. The shared Map dropdown also provides a top-down view. Cross-section plots and the curve profile are omitted as requested.
- **Arrows:** All gradient arrows lie in the xy plane, with actual magnitudes at the same common scale in both views. Objective arrows are solid amber; constraint arrows are dashed green. No lifted or chosen-direction arrows. A two-item legend pairs each contour family with its gradient.
- **Controls:** Position angle follows the ellipse; Minimum point and Maximum point select exact candidates. Dragging either 3D map rotates both without moving the point; arrow keys rotate the focused maps. Dragging the top-down map moves the point. No direction controls or Settings panel.
- **Text:** Point, objective, and both gradients remain visible. Exact integer results have no approximation mark; other outputs use the shared three-significant-digit formatter. Only extrema show brief feedback, with the shared green completion effect at the minimum. Instructions are confined to the initially closed Info panel.
- **History and panels:** Shared Undo/Redo/Reset. One drag, slider gesture, or held rotation key is one action; cancellation restores its prior state. Reset restores the starting point while preserving map and camera. Brief Info remains open during outside interaction and closes with Escape. No progress saving.
- **Drag protection:** Maps disable text selection (including Safari), native image dragging, and touch scrolling within the plot. Accepted pointer gestures prevent browser defaults and capture the pointer. Article text and Info remain selectable.
- **Fallback and checks:** Three map previews come from the shared model; the identifying mark is an outlined STIX lambda.
