## Organization notes

Logarithmic and implicit differentiation share a module. Inverse-function derivatives and hyperbolic functions belong with derivative foundations. Indeterminate forms and l’Hôpital’s rule belong under derivative applications. Both Fundamental Theorem parts stay with integral foundations. The schedule’s “inverse trigonometric substitution” is labeled “Trigonometric substitution.” Only the Limits submodule has been authored so far.
