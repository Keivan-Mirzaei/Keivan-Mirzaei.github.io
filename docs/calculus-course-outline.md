## Organization notes

Logarithmic and implicit differentiation share a module. Inverse-function derivatives and hyperbolic functions belong with derivative foundations. Indeterminate forms and l’Hôpital’s rule belong under derivative applications. Both Fundamental Theorem parts stay with integral foundations.
